Using Catalan administrative data and a regression discontinuity design, we evaluate Spain's 2019
reform extending the minimum rental lease from three to five or seven years. Lease lengths and
medium-run tenant stability both rise, but the adjustment differs sharply by landlord type:
individual landlords absorb the change, while some corporate landlords exit the market.
