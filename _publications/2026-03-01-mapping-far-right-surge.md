Exploiting mid-20th century internal migration flows from Spanish municipalities to Catalonia, we
show that places with a higher historical propensity to send migrants to Catalonia have lower VOX
vote shares in the 2019 election. Survey evidence on personal ties to Catalonia points to the same
pattern, extending contact theory to indirect contact through durable social networks.
